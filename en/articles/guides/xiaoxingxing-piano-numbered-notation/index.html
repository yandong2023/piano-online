Twinkle Twinkle Little Star: Numbered Piano Notation for Beginners

Start with this C-major numbered melody for the right hand. Match each number to a piano note, then play the six phrases in A, A, B, B, A, A order. This is a single melody, not a two-hand arrangement.

You will learn
  • Read 1=C and scale degrees

01

How to read the numbers: 1=C, 4/4 time

1=C means C is do. Numbers 1 through 7 mean C, D, E, F, G, A, B. This melody uses only 1 through 6. Start at middle C (C4) and use the white keys.

Each number below lasts one beat; an em dash extends the previous note by one beat. A bar line separates measures of four beats. These numbers are scale degrees, not finger numbers or computer keys.

02

Complete numbered notation: all six phrases

Phrase 1 (A): 1 1 5 5 | 6 6 5 — |

Phrase 2 (A): 4 4 3 3 | 2 2 1 — |

Phrase 3 (B): 5 5 4 4 | 3 3 2 — |

Phrase 4 (B): 5 5 4 4 | 3 3 2 — |

Phrase 5 (A): 1 1 5 5 | 6 6 5 — |

Phrase 6 (A): 4 4 3 3 | 2 2 1 — |

Practice tip

Each phrase has seven notes over eight beats. Hold its final 5, 1 or 2 for two beats. Phrases three and four match; five and six repeat the opening.

03

Play the opening phrase on a computer keyboard

On this site, K plays C4, W plays G4 and E plays A4. The opening 1 1 5 5 6 6 5 becomes K K W W E E W. On a phone, tap the piano keys.

04

Practise phrases, then connect the melody

  1. 1

    Start at 60 beats per minute: one beat per number, two for the final note.

  2. 2

    Practise phrase two descending from 4 to 1, then connect the first two phrases.

  3. 3

    Repeat the middle phrase twice, descending from 5 to 2.

  4. 4

    Finish by repeating the opening two phrases. Once the pulse is steady, try the melody from memory.

Practice tip

Keep a steady pulse before adding any left-hand accompaniment. Use the related interactive song to find each key.
