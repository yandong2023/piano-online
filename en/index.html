What is a virtual piano?

A virtual piano is an online piano keyboard you can play in your browser. This free visual piano lets you use a mouse, touchscreen or computer keys without downloading software. Prefer Chinese? Open 在线钢琴.

Virtual piano with notes

Musical notes such as C4 name the pitch; computer-key labels tell you what to press here. K plays middle C (C4). Explore the virtual piano with notes for labeled keys.

How to play

Click a piano key to enable sound. On a computer, press its matching key label. Switch from Free Play to Learn a Song when you want to follow highlighted notes. For MIDI playback, open Piano MIDI online.

NEW TO PIANO?

You Can Start Without Reading Sheet Music

You do not need to read sheet music before playing your first melody. Start with highlighted keys, then compare each note with its name and computer-key label. These are simplified melodies, not full two-hand piano arrangements.

How do I play on a computer?

Click a key, or press its large letter. A plays C3, K plays middle C (C4), and T plays C5. Numbers and some letters play the black keys.

How do I play on a phone?

Use the arrows below the keys to move the range. Guided mode brings the next key into view automatically. Landscape gives you more playing space.

Why can’t I hear anything?

Tap a piano key to allow browser audio. Check media volume, silent mode, Bluetooth output and the volume slider. A synthesized tone is used if samples cannot load.

Virtual piano questions

Can I play piano online for free?

Yes. You can play this virtual piano in your browser without creating an account or downloading an app.

Can I use my computer keyboard to play piano?

Yes. Press the computer-key label shown on each piano key. On PianoOnline, K plays middle C (C4), and W plays G4. You can also play with a mouse or touchscreen.

How do I learn a song if I cannot read sheet music?

Choose Learn a Song, select a melody, and follow the highlighted piano keys. Each song page also shows musical note names and matching computer-key labels.

What is the difference between a piano note and a computer key?

A piano note such as C4 names the sound. A computer key such as K is what you press on this website. Some mapped keys use numbers or punctuation, so follow the labels.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Tap the on-screen keys and move the visible keyboard range using the on-screen controls. Landscape usually gives you more room.

Why is there no sound?

Click or tap a piano key to enable browser audio. Then check your device volume, silent mode, Bluetooth output and the piano volume control.

Is this a piano simulator or a real 88-key piano?

It is a browser-based piano simulator with 37 playable keys (C3–C6). You can practise melodies and note positions, but it is not a physical 88-key piano.

Does every song include sheet music?

No. Song pages provide note names and computer-key labels. Some also include interactive staff notation. These are simplified practice melodies.
