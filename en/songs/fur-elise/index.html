About the Für Elise melody

What are the first notes of Für Elise?

It starts with E D# E D# E B D C.

Does Für Elise use black keys?

Yes: D#, G#. Each # is the black key just to the right of that letter.

What range does it cover?

From C4 (middle C) to F5. Everything fits on the 37-key piano above (C3–C6).

How long does it take to learn?

This version has 63 notes at about 130 BPM. Most beginners can play it through in about 6 minutes of guided practice.
