About the Happy Birthday melody

What are the first notes of Happy Birthday?

It starts with G G A G C B G G.

Does Happy Birthday use black keys?

No. This simplified melody uses only white keys, which makes it a comfortable first song.

What range does it cover?

From G4 to G5. Everything fits on the 37-key piano above (C3–C6).

How long does it take to learn?

This version has 25 notes at about 120 BPM. Most beginners can play it through in about 3 minutes of guided practice.
